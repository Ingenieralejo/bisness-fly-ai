# 🧠 FLY.AI MASTER NEURAL TRAINING PLAN — "THE GLOBAL ARCHITECT"

## 🏁 MISSION OBJECTIVE
Transform the Local Swarm from general purpose to **Global Expert Level** in Finance, Accounting, and International Markets. Inject 15 years of market intelligence to ensure autonomous ROI maximization.

## 🧱 KNOWLEDGE PILLARS
The following domains will be "hardcoded" into the Agent System Prompts and the Local RAG (Knowledge Base):

### 1. International Business & Trade
- **Global Strategy**: Trade routes, import/export incoterms, international contract templates.
- **Logistics Ops**: Supply chain optimization logic for Dropshipping and B2B.

### 2. Accounting & Financial Engineering
- **Neural Accounting**: Automated P&L (Profit & Loss), Cash Flow projection, and Tax strategy.
- **Advanced Math**: Implementation of Game Theory, Monte Carlo simulations for risk, and Pareto distributions for lead scanning.

### 3. FX & Stock Market Intelligence (15-Year Horizon)
- **Macro Sentiment**: Correlation between interest rates (FED/ECB) and currency fluctuations.
- **Historical Benchmarks**: Mapping the 2011-2026 market behavior (Bull/Bear cycles, Flash crashes, AI booms).

## 🏗️ INFRASTRUCTURE: THE KNOWLEDGE VAULT

## 📈 15-YEAR MARKET DATA STRATEGY
We cannot download 10TB of raw tick data. Instead, we will inject **"Compressed Market Intelligence"**:
- **Trend Matrices**: Summaries of each year's key market drivers.
- **Volatility Maps**: Identifying high-risk/high-reward periods historically.
- **Correlation Tables**: How Currencies (USD/EUR/COP) respond to commodities (Oil/Gold).
